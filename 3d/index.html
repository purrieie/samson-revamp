Description

  • Dynamic big toe alignment support: designed to gently support proper big toe alignment and help maintain a more natural toe position while walking, standing, and during daily activities.
  • Adjustable fit and secure support: adjustable straps let you customise the fit and tension, for secure, targeted support around the big toe and foot without feeling overly restrictive.
  • Soft, skin-friendly comfort: made with soft, cushioned fabric that feels comfortable against the skin while allowing the foot to move naturally.
  • Hinged design and controlled movement: a dynamic hinge supports the big toe while allowing controlled movement as you go about your day.

How to wear it

  1. Slide the Bunion Corrector onto your foot and position it comfortably around the big toe joint.
  2. Make sure the hinge joint sits directly over the big toe joint.
  3. Adjust the larger mid-foot strap and the smaller toe strap for a secure and comfortable fit.
  4. Wear it during daily activities, walking, or whenever you want extra big toe alignment support.
  5. Do not wear it excessively tight. Remove it immediately if you feel numbness, irritation, or discomfort.
  6. Start with shorter periods and increase wearing time gradually, as comfortable.

Care

Hand wash separately in cold water. Do not bleach, iron, or dry clean. Air dry away from direct heat or sunlight. Avoid wringing, twisting, or machine drying.

FAQs

Will the Bunion Corrector slip down while walking?

No, the adjustable straps help keep it securely in place. Make sure the straps are tightened comfortably before walking.

Can I wear the Bunion Corrector on both feet?

Yes, the one-size design can be used on either the left or right foot. Simply adjust the straps for a comfortable fit.

Will it restrict the movement of my big toe?

No, the dynamic hinge allows controlled movement of the big toe while providing alignment support.

Can I wear it with shoes?

Yes, but choose footwear with enough room so the corrector doesn't feel tight or cause pressure on your foot.
